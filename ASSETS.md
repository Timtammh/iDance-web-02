No iDance logo is applied to these concepts. `fallback.svg`: missing-image fallback. Text wordmark is a temporary brand label, not a recreated official logo.

## Temporary editorial photography

Unsplash image URLs are listed in `src/data/demo.ts`. They are generic music, family, friends and studio scenes, not evidence of iDance product use or customer testimonials. Replace with approved/licensed brand assets; verify rights/releases for your exact commercial use. Remote delivery requires internet and fails gracefully to a local image. ## Private-preview synchronized mixer (2026-11-11)

The Drums, Bass, Chords and Lead pads use the four WAV stems from **Arcade — Arcade Loop**, by Oğuzhan Girgin / Colorosse. Source: https://www.colorosse.com/assets/audio/music/arcade-music-loop. License: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. The publisher's bundled license and README are retained under `public/audio/arcade/`. The license expressly permits commercial use, modification and redistribution; attribution is not required. Download verified 2026-11-11. Four stems are each 278,528 samples, 44.1 kHz mono PCM16 (6.315827664 seconds), sharing the same four-bar timeline in A Aeolian at approximately 152 BPM. No MP3 encoder padding is involved.

Clap, Hi-hat, Perc and Spark are supplemental original procedural accents generated by `deckAudio.ts`, not additional third-party samples. They are rendered to the same duration and sample rate, then played on the same transport. Pitch/tempo changes apply equally to every layer to retain alignment. This is website demo audio, not a representation of the hardware's built-in sounds.
